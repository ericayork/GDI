Source. U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis, National Income and Product Accounts, Table 1.10, Gross Domestic Income by Type of Income (billions of dollars). Shares are computed by category as a percentage of Gross Domestic Income, except the final view, which uses net income.

Method. Employee compensation combines wages, salaries, and benefits. Government combines net taxes on production and imports with corporate income taxes. Depreciation is the consumption of fixed capital. The current surplus of government enterprises is excluded from both denominators (GDI and net income); it is not separately reported before 1959, when it was netted into subsidies. Net income removes depreciation and business taxes; labor and capital shares are measured against it. Inspired by Tax Foundation, "A Walkthrough of Gross Domestic Income."
